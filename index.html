The Mongol Empire

How big did it get?

The Mongol Empire started in Mongolia and grew into the biggest empire made of connected land in history. Click a year to see how it grew, then click the yellow dots to learn about important places.

Quick facts

  • Genghis Khan became leader of the Mongols in 1206.
  • At its biggest the empire was about 24 million square kilometres.
  • The Mongols had a fast mail system called the Yam so messages could travel far.
  • After 1294 the empire split into four smaller parts called khanates.

Why it matters

With the Mongols controlling so much land, trade on the Silk Road was safer, so goods, ideas and inventions moved between Asia and Europe. But the invasions also destroyed cities and killed a lot of people.
